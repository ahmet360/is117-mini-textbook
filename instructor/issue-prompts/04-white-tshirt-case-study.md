# Issue 4 — The Plain White T-Shirt Case Study

## Objective

Synthesize persuasion, archetype, and design language.

## Required Output

`book/04-white-tshirt-case-study.md`

## Acceptance Criteria

Create at least four distinctly positioned versions of the same plain white T-shirt.

Each version must specify:

- target audience
- brand archetype
- persuasive principles
- visual language
- headline
- short product story
- likely imagery
- ethical risk or limitation

Include a synthesis table and one Mermaid diagram.

## Prompt to Give Your AI Assistant

Create `book/04-white-tshirt-case-study.md`.

Use one imaginary physical product: an ordinary high-quality plain white T-shirt.

Create at least four radically different product presentations while keeping the physical shirt substantially the same.

For each concept, specify:
- audience
- chosen brand archetype
- persuasion principles being used
- visual-design language
- headline
- short product story
- imagery direction
- what meaning the customer is actually being invited to buy
- ethical concern or possible misuse

At least one concept should use an Explorer archetype.

At least one should use a restrained modernist / Swiss-influenced design language.

At least one should use a disruptive or postmodern visual language.

Include a synthesis table comparing all concepts and a valid Mermaid diagram showing how Product + Audience + Meaning + Persuasion + Visual Language combine into a market presentation.

Make the chapter entertaining enough that a student would actually want to read it.

Do not fabricate external facts or citations.

Finish with `What You Should Remember`.
